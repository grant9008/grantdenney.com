What it does

Everything in one addon

Prices on every item

  • Big call: Auction it or Vendor it, counting the AH deposit
  • AH value vs the usual price: "+88% vs usual: spike"
  • Sell now: 1c under the cheapest listing
  • Someone dumping? "38% under usual" and a Sell at price
  • Stack worth after the AH cut; live prices at the AH

At the auction house

  • Price chart under the AH: the last day, week or month
  • Sell now / Sell at buttons fill the price (gear: whole silver); posting stays your click
  • Tiny up/down arrows in the gold, silver, copper boxes
  • No results? When it was last listed, for how much; craft costs
  • Sidebar: your watchlist and flip ideas (experimental, up to 20g)

Enchant tips

  • On the gear you wear: Top 5 (or Top 2), mats and AH cost
  • Your role from your talents; kits, scopes, blacksmith items too
  • Speed boots keep their stat options
  • Best already on? One grey line says so
  • A tick box on the character screen turns them off

Consumes

  • Under your character screen: food, potions, elixirs, scrolls, flasks
  • "ELIXIRS - 3 at once"; a grey or row is one to take instead
  • Food and flasks: 1 at a time; hover a title for the stacking rule
  • Price and % of the best on every row
  • Best, Budget (cheaper picks) or All

Flight timer

  • Flight map: hover a place to see its flight time
  • Flying: a big countdown up top; the strip is time left
  • Drag it anywhere, it stays; X hides it till you land

Boat timer

  • At a dock: a big countdown, or "docked, leaves in 0:35"
  • Learns from your rides and guild mates with the addon
  • Unknown? "every 5:29" and a "Click when the boat docks" button
  • Drag it anywhere, it stays; X hides it till you leave

Crafting profit

  • Plans and patterns: mats cost and profit after the AH cut
  • Items your recipes make: their mats and what they cost
  • Ore and bars: what your own recipes make of them
  • One line in the Professions window too
  • Crafted items priced at the tooltip's Sell now

Bag cleanup

  • A Bag cleanup button on your bags (ElvUI too)
  • Cheapest stacks first, stacks to merge
  • Drop cheapest always asks first
  • At a vendor: Sell junk, or click a row to sell it
  • Quest items say "Can drop" or "Keep" (best with Questie)

Price website

  • Price history for every item, on your phone too
  • Favorites lists, plus auto lists of the most traded greens, blues and epics

Install

Install in 3 minutes

  1. Download

    GoblinLedger.zip

    Browser asks? Choose Keep (Edge: Keep anyway).

  2. Unzip it

    Right-click the zip, Extract All, Extract.

    Keep that folder: its installer also gets fresh prices.

  3. Run the installer

    Double-click Install-Goblin-Ledger.bat.

    Windows warns? More info, Run anyway.

    Game not found? It shows how to paste your _forever_ folder.

    Two questions: press Enter for each.

    Yes: prices every 30 min, new versions by themselves.

  4. Start WoW

    Was it open? Close it fully first.

    Hover an item: the prices are there.

Commands

Type these in chat

  • /gl help every command
  • /reload load the new prices
  • /gl compact one-line tooltips
  • /gl chart Linen Cloth chart any item
  • /gl watch Runecloth add to watchlist
  • /gl flips flip ideas in chat
  • /gl bags cleanup list in chat
  • /gl boat now boat just docked: learn it
  • /gl update your version, how to update
  • Shift hold: whole stack, every mat
More commands
  • /gl tooltip prices off or on
  • /gl sidebar AH sidebar away or back
  • /gl keep Runecloth bag cleanup skips it
  • /gl enchants top2 best two only
  • /gl enchants tank pick your role
  • /gl consumes budget cheaper picks
  • /gl boats small line under the minimap
  • /gl boats big big again
  • /gl flights small a slim flight bar
  • /gl flights big big again
  • /gl boats reset back to the middle
  • /gl flights reset same for flights

Turn a part off: /gl boats off, and on brings it back. Works for enchants, consumes, crafts, flights and boats (enchants come back with auto, which also reads your role from talents).

Help

Good to know

Updates

  • Prices update by themselves every 30 min; /reload loads them
  • 3+ hours old? They get a ~ and the tooltip says what to do
  • New versions install by themselves (if you said yes)
  • Or a chat line says one is out: /gl update says how
  • Had 1.0.0? Download the zip again and run its installer once

Safe and simple

  • Never buys, bids or posts; buttons only fill the price box
  • Sells or drops only when you click; dropping asks first
  • Works with ElvUI, Auctionator and Questie; no scanning
  • Alliance, US PvP realms; Windows only

Not working?

  • Close WoW fully and start it again
  • Character select screen: AddOns, tick Goblin Ledger
  • Can't copy? Right-click the installer, Run as administrator

To remove it: Uninstall-Goblin-Ledger.bat.
