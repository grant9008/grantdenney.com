What it does

Everything in one addon

Prices on every item

  • Big call: Auction it, Vendor it, Close call or Maybe auction
  • AH value vs usual, and Sell now: 1c under the cheapest
  • Someone dumping? It says "52% under usual" and gives a Sell at

Bag cleanup

  • A Bag cleanup button on your bags (ElvUI too)
  • Cheapest stacks, old quest items, stacks to merge
  • Never deletes on its own: dropping always asks first

At the auction house

  • A price chart under the AH: 1D, 5D, 1W, 1M, 1Y
  • Sell now button fills the price; arrows nudge it
  • Empty search? Last listed, sold a day, mats cost of plans

Enchant tips

  • On the gear you wear (character screen)
  • Best enchants for your class and role, mats and AH cost
  • Top 5 by default; the Top 2 button shows just the best

Crafting profit

  • Plans, patterns, ore and bars show what the mats cost
  • Green when crafting or smelting pays after the AH cut
  • One line in the Professions window too

Consumes

  • Hangs under your character screen
  • Best food, potions, elixirs, scrolls for your role and level
  • Prices, % of best, and a Best / Budget switch

Flight timer

  • The flight map shows each flight's time
  • A slim countdown bar while you fly; its X hides it

Boat timer

  • At a dock: when the boat or zeppelin comes
  • Learns from your rides; X hides it until you leave

Price website

  • Price history charts for every item
  • Favorites lists; works on your phone

Install

Install in 3 minutes

  1. Download

    GoblinLedger.zip

    Browser asks? Choose Keep (Edge: Keep anyway).

  2. Unzip it

    Right-click the zip, Extract All, Extract.

  3. Run the installer

    Double-click Install-Goblin-Ledger.bat, press Y twice.

    Windows warns? More info, Run anyway.

  4. Restart WoW

    Hover an item: the prices are there.

Commands

Type these in chat

  • /gl help every command
  • /reload load the newest prices
  • /gl compact one-line tooltips
  • /gl tooltip tooltip prices off or on
  • /gl chart Linen Cloth a chart for any item
  • /gl bags the bag cleanup list in chat
  • /gl enchants top2 just the best two (top5: five)
  • /gl enchants tank pick your role (auto: talents)
  • /gl consumes budget cheaper picks (best: strongest)
  • /gl crafts off hide the craft tips
  • /gl flights off hide the flight timer
  • /gl boat now as a boat docks: sets its timer
  • /gl boats off hide the boat timer
  • /gl update your version, how to update
  • Shift hold over a stack or plan: whole stack, every mat

Help

Good to know

Updates

  • Prices update every 30 min by themselves
  • The game loads them at log in or /reload
  • New versions install themselves. On 1.0.0? Run the new installer once.

Safe and simple

  • Never buys, bids or posts for you
  • Alliance, US PvP realms; Windows only
  • Unlike Auctionator, no scanning; runs fine next to it

Not working?

  • Close WoW fully and start it again
  • Character screen, AddOns: tick Goblin Ledger
  • Can't copy? Right-click the installer, Run as administrator

To remove it: Uninstall-Goblin-Ledger.bat.
