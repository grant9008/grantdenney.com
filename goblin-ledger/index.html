Goblin Ledger

Auction house prices right in your item tooltips, so you know what to auction, what to vendor and what to flip.

A free addon and price website for WoW Forever. One Windows installer, about 3 minutes, no sign-up and no Battle.net key.

Download for Windows See the price website

GoblinLedger.zip, free, from GitHub. Works alongside ElvUI, Auctionator and Questie.

What you get

Prices wherever you need them

In your tooltips, in your bags, at the auction house and on the web. All of it runs on the same prices, refreshed about every hour.

Prices in your tooltips

Hover an item and the tooltip shows:

  • AH value: what it goes for on the auction house now, with a green or red % against its recent average
  • Average: its average price over the last 7 days
  • Sell now: 1 copper under the cheapest listing
  • Suggested price: what it usually sells for, going by its price history
  • A big green Auction it or red Vendor it that says how much better that choice is, like "Auction it +26% vs vendor". When it is not clear-cut, it says Close call or Maybe auction.

Tip: hold Shift over a stack to see what the whole stack is worth.

Bag cleanup

Bags full again? A gold Bag cleanup button sits on your bag window, on the game's own bags or on ElvUI's. Click it to see:

  • Worth the least: your stacks, cheapest first, at the better of the vendor price and the auction price
  • Old quest items: quest items no quest in your log asks for, and items that start a quest you already finished
  • Merge stacks: partial stacks that fit in one slot
  • Sell junk: at a vendor, sells your grey items for you
  • Sell a stack: at a vendor, click a stack under Worth the least to sell it right away (you can buy it back from the vendor)
  • Drop cheapest: asks first, naming the stack and what it is worth

Nothing is sold or destroyed without your click, and Rare or better items always go through the game's own check. Your hearthstone, tools, reagents, ammo and keys never show up on the list.

Help at the auction house

  • A price chart docks under the auction house window and follows the item you search for or click.
  • Sell now and Suggested buttons fill in the price box. You still click Post yourself.
  • A sidebar of flip ideas sized to the gold on your character, ranked by the profit you can expect within a day. Click one to search for it.
  • Your watchlist: a starter list of trade goods that you add to and trim.

It never buys, bids, posts or cancels anything for you.

The price website

Open grantdenney.com/goblin-ledger/prices on your PC or phone:

  • Browse every item on the auction house
  • Price history charts for each item
  • Flip Finder with deals on greens, blues and epics
  • Favorites lists for the items you care about

Prices come from Blizzard's auction house data for the Alliance auction house on the US PvP realms, refreshed about every hour.

Install

Install in 3 minutes

Windows only. All you need is WoW Forever installed on your PC.

  1. Download the zip

    Download GoblinLedger.zip

    Your browser may check with you first. The file is new, so not many people have downloaded it yet. You may see:

    Edge: "GoblinLedger.zip isn't commonly downloaded"

    Point at the download, click the three dots ... and choose Keep. In the box that opens, click Show more, then Keep anyway.

    Chrome: asks if you want to keep the file

    Choose Keep.

  2. Unzip it

    In your Downloads folder, right-click GoblinLedger.zip, choose Extract All, then Extract. The new folder holds:

    • GoblinLedger the addon
    • Install-Goblin-Ledger.bat the installer
    • Uninstall-Goblin-Ledger.bat removes it again
    • README.txt these steps in short

    Unzip first: the installer does not work from inside the zip.

  3. Run the installer

    Double-click Install-Goblin-Ledger.bat. A black window opens and sets everything up:

    • finds WoW Forever in C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_, or asks you where it is if your game is somewhere else
    • copies the addon into Interface\AddOns\GoblinLedger
    • downloads the latest prices
    • adds a Windows task called Goblin Ledger Prices that quietly refreshes the prices every 30 minutes while you are logged in to Windows (no window pops up, and Uninstall-Goblin-Ledger.bat removes it)

    If it asks whether to update prices automatically, press Y.

    Windows may warn you first. It does that for files from the internet that are not from a big-name publisher. You may see one or both of these:

    A blue box: "Windows protected your PC"

    Click More info, then Run anyway.

    A box called "Open File - Security Warning"

    It asks if you want to run the file. Click Run.

    Rather skip the warnings? Before you unzip, right-click GoblinLedger.zip, choose Properties, tick Unblock at the bottom and click OK. Curious what the installer does? It is plain text: right-click it and choose Edit (on Windows 11, Show more options first) to read every line in Notepad.

    Game in another folder? In the Battle.net app, click the gear next to Play and choose Show in Explorer. Go into the _forever_ folder if you are not already in it. Click the address bar at the top and press Ctrl+C. Then click the installer window, press Ctrl+V (or right-click) and press Enter.

  4. Start the game

    Start WoW Forever. Was it already open? Close it fully and start it again: the game only notices a new addon when it starts.

    Hover something you could sell, like cloth or ore. See the Goblin Ledger lines? You are done. Type /gl help for the list of commands.

    You can delete the zip and the unzipped folder now. Need the installer or the uninstaller again later? Just download the zip again.

Using it

Quick tips

It all works on its own. These help when you want more.

/reload

Prices load when you log in. Type /reload to pick up fresh ones without logging out.

/gl help

Shows every command in one list.

/gl bags

What to drop or sell first, in chat. Handy if you use another bag addon.

/gl flips

The current flip ideas, sized to the gold you are carrying.

/gl watch <item>

Adds an item to your auction house watchlist, like /gl watch Runecloth. You can shift-click the item into chat instead of typing its name.

/gl compact

Switches tooltips between the full view and a single line.

/gl tooltip

Turns the prices in tooltips off, or back on.

Hold Shift

Over a stack, the tooltip prices count the whole stack.

Sell now and Suggested

They only fill in the price box. Check the price, then click Post.

Later on

Updating and removing

Nothing to babysit. Here is what to do when you want to.

Fresh prices

They update by themselves every 30 minutes while you are logged in to Windows. The game picks them up when you log in or type /reload.

Want fresh prices this minute? Run Install-Goblin-Ledger.bat again.

A new version

Download the zip again from this page, unzip it and run Install-Goblin-Ledger.bat. It replaces the old addon files. Then restart the game.

Removing it

Double-click Uninstall-Goblin-Ledger.bat, in the folder you unzipped. Deleted that folder? Download the zip again and unzip it. If it asks you to confirm, press Y. It removes the addon and the Goblin Ledger Prices task.

By hand: delete the GoblinLedger folder from your game's Interface\AddOns folder, then delete the Goblin Ledger Prices task in Task Scheduler.

FAQ

Questions

The short answers.

Which realms does it cover?

Alliance on the US (North America) PvP realms. That is the one auction house the prices come from today.

It installs on any character, but on Horde, PvE, Hardcore or another region your auction house is a different market, so the prices will not match yours. On Horde and Hardcore the tooltip leaves out the Auction it / Vendor it advice for that reason.

Is it safe?

Yes. Most of it only shows information. It never buys, bids, posts or cancels anything: the Sell now and Suggested buttons only fill in the price box. It sells or drops items only when you click. Sell junk, or a click on a stack in the Bag cleanup list at a vendor, sells right away (you can buy it back from the vendor), and dropping an item always asks first.

The installer is a plain text file you can read in Notepad. It copies the addon into your AddOns folder, downloads the public price file from GitHub and adds one Windows task that keeps that file fresh. No passwords, no Battle.net key or account, and nothing about you or your PC is sent anywhere.

Do I need anything else?

No. Just WoW Forever on a Windows PC with an internet connection. No other addons, no sign-up, no Battle.net key.

Does it work with ElvUI, Auctionator and Questie?

  • ElvUI: yes. The Bag cleanup button shows up in ElvUI's bag window.
  • Auctionator: yes. The price chart follows Auctionator's tabs, and the Sell now and Suggested buttons sit under its price box in the Selling tab.
  • Questie: yes, they run side by side. Bag cleanup reads your own quest log to spot old quest items.

Another bag addon that hides the game's bags? Type /gl bags to get the cleanup list in chat.

How fresh are the prices?

Blizzard updates its auction house data about once an hour, and the price file follows soon after. Your PC fetches it every 30 minutes, and the game loads it when you log in or type /reload. The tooltip shows how old your prices are.

Does it work on a Mac?

Not yet. The installer and the automatic price updates are Windows only. The price website works on any computer or phone.

It shows no prices. What now?

  1. Close WoW completely and start it again. A new addon only loads when the game starts.
  2. At the character screen, click AddOns and check that Goblin Ledger is ticked. If it says out of date, tick Load out of date AddOns.
  3. Type /gl in chat. No answer means the addon is not loaded: run the installer again and check it found the right game folder.
  4. The addon says there is no price data yet? Run Install-Goblin-Ledger.bat again, then type /reload.
  5. The installer said Windows would not let it copy into the game folder? Right-click Install-Goblin-Ledger.bat and choose Run as administrator.

Soulbound and most quest items cannot go on the auction house, so they show no price, or a price without the Auction it / Vendor it call. Items nobody has listed lately say "no recent listings".

Still stuck? Send a screenshot of the installer window to whoever sent you this page.
