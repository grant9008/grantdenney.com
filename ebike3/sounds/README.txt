YOUR OWN SOUNDS FOR E-BIKE SPEEDRUNNER
======================================

You can replace the rider's built-in crash scream (and the OOF when he lands, and people yelling) with your own
recordings. No code, just files in this folder.

THE EASIEST WAY: THE SCREAM BOOTH (IN THE GAME)
-----------------------------------------------
Open the gear menu (or the crash card) and tap SCREAM BOOTH. Say anything for 2-4 seconds ("HEYYY", your name,
"BANANAAA") or upload a clip. The game turns it into 3 screams (whimper, scream, blood-curdling) plus OOFs from
your own voice. Turn on USE MINE. Your voice stays on that phone only. It is never uploaded.
  - Over Wi-Fi from the PC, the iPhone blocks the microphone (an iPhone rule for plain "http" links), so use
    UPLOAD there: record in Voice Memos, then Share > Save to Files, then pick it in the booth.
  - RECORD works in the App Store version of the game, and on the PC.

THE FOLDER WAY (THIS FOLDER)
----------------------------
1. Record on your iPhone with the Voice Memos app. Scream your heart out.
2. Share the recording to the PC (AirDrop doesn't reach Windows: email it to yourself, or use iCloud Drive,
   OneDrive or Google Drive). You get a file like "New Recording 3.m4a".
3. Rename it and drop it in this folder (public/sounds):
     scream-1.m4a   the scream while you fly through the air
     oof-1.m4a      the grunt when you smack into something
     yell-1.m4a     a person yelling at you (drivers, pedestrians)
   Make as many as you like: scream-2.m4a, scream-3.m4a ... The game picks a different one each time.
   .m4a, .mp3 and .wav all work. Keep each clip short (a scream 2-4 s, an oof under 1 s, a yell 1-2 s), and trim
   the silence at the start in Voice Memos (Edit > Trim) so it starts right away.
4. Reload the game. That's it. (When the game is built for the App Store, every file here is included
   automatically.)

To go back to the built-in sounds, delete or rename your files (for example "old-scream-1.m4a").
If the Scream Booth's USE MINE is on, the booth voice wins over the files in this folder.
