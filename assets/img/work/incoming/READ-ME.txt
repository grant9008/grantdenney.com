Drop project screenshots in this folder, then double-click Add-Screenshots.bat
in the main site folder.

Name each file after the project it belongs to:

    pocketge.png       ->  the PocketGE card
    fliptracker.png    ->  the PocketGE Flip Tracker card
    iracehud.png       ->  the iRaceHUD card
    tcrescue.png       ->  the Town & Country Animal Rescue card

.png, .jpg and .webp all work. Bigger is better - they get resized and
compressed automatically. Wide shots (roughly 3:2 or 16:9) fit the panel best.

You don't have to do them all at once. Anything you haven't supplied keeps
its placeholder panel until you do.
