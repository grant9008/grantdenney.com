SEND IT: Red Light Runner: Help

SEND IT: Red Light Runner is an arcade speedrun game for iPhone: one kid, four rides, two busy boulevards. This page answers the usual questions and says how to reach us.

Contact: gdenns@gmail.com. We read every message and try to answer within a few days. Please say which iPhone you have and what you were doing when something went wrong.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 16.4 or newer.
  • The game plays with the phone held upright or sideways, whichever you like (it follows the phone), and works without an internet connection.
  • It is free. There are no ads and nothing to buy in this version. There is no account.

How to play

  • On the home screen, tap CAREER (the biggest button) for your next career goal, or TIME TRIAL, where you tap TAP TO RIDE. On the same screen you pick the level (HOLLYWOOD BLVD or SUNSET BLVD), the ride and the traffic: CHILL, RUSH HOUR or MAYHEM. All three traffic modes are open from the start; SUNSET BLVD opens in TIME TRIAL once your career reaches it.
  • You start on the WOBBLE BOARD. The other rides are earned on Hollywood: finish on the board for the E-SCOOTER, win a bronze medal on it for the COMFORT CRUISER, then a bronze on the cruiser for the E-MOTO. A bronze in any traffic mode counts, and so do 5 finishes on the ride before (the NEXT RIDE line in TIME TRIAL shows the count). A locked ride's button shows a lock and its speed; BOARDS says what the next ride needs. A locked SUNSET BLVD says which career stage opens it.
  • Left thumb: the two arrows steer.
  • Right thumb: hold GO. BRAKE is right beside it.
  • Jumps and rail grinds happen by themselves. In the air, press TRICK (above GO). Landed tricks fill the SEND IT meter; a full meter is a speed boost. On the cruiser and the E-MOTO, hold TRICK on the ground for a wheelie.
  • RETRY (top of the screen) starts again at any moment.
  • The gear at the top opens SETTINGS: sound and voice switches, the kid's lines (ALL / CLEAN / OFF), how crashes are shown (FULL / SKIPPABLE / INSTANT RETRY), the minimap, the leaderboards, the Scream Booth and CLIPS.

Questions

There is no sound.

The game follows the iPhone's Ring/Silent switch: on SILENT it is quiet. Check the switch and the volume buttons, then SETTINGS > ALL SOUND.

I do not like the kid's jokes.

SETTINGS > KID LINES: CLEAN drops the insult jokes, OFF drops his lines from the crash and finish cards.

I do not want to watch the crashes.

SETTINGS > CRASHES: SKIPPABLE gives every crash a SKIP button; INSTANT RETRY restarts the moment you crash.

What is the Scream Booth, and where does my recording go?

It records up to four seconds of your voice and turns it into the kid's crash scream. iOS asks for the microphone only when you tap RECORD. The recording stays on your iPhone and is never uploaded. DELETE MINE in the Scream Booth erases it. You can take the microphone permission back at any time: the iPhone's Settings app, Apps > SEND IT > Microphone (on iOS 17 and older: Settings > SEND IT).

What does CLIPS do?

After a crash, CLIPS makes an upright video of it (or of another moment of your runs) on your iPhone. SHARE opens the iPhone's own share sheet; where the video goes from there is your choice. If you choose Save Video, iOS asks once for permission to add it to your Photos. The game itself uploads nothing.

CLIPS says the video has no sound.

The sound part of a clip needs iOS 26 or newer. On older systems the clip is made without sound.

The game asked me "HELP FIX THE GAME?". What is that?

It asks whether the game may send anonymous play numbers (runs, crashes, finish times, frame rate, error messages) to a service called GameAnalytics, under a random id. Nothing is sent unless you tap SURE. You can change your answer at any time: SETTINGS > ANALYTICS. Our privacy policy says exactly what is sent: https://grantdenney.com/sendit/privacy/

My best times are gone.

Times, medals and settings are stored on your iPhone; the game never restores them from a server. Deleting the game deletes them, and they do not move to a new phone. If they vanished without that, please write to us with your iPhone model and iOS version.

My world rank.

If a result card asked "WORLD BOARD: SHOW YOUR BEST TIMES, TRIES AND CRASHES TO OTHER PLAYERS?", or BOARDS has a WORLD list, the game has a world board: one world list for each level, traffic mode and ride. It asks before it sends anything about you. After YES, your best times, your three initials and how often you tried and crashed are kept by our rank service under a random id, and other players see them on the lists. In SETTINGS (the gear), WORLD BOARD ON / OFF changes your answer and ERASE MY ONLINE TIMES deletes what is online. Rude initials or an impossible time on a list: tap its flag to report it; it disappears from your iPhone at once, and we check every report within a day. If you deleted the game since, email us the level, the traffic mode, the ride, your initials and the time.

The game is slow or the phone gets warm.

Close other apps, and check that Low Power Mode is off (iPhone Settings > Battery): in Low Power Mode iOS itself holds games like this one to 30 pictures a second. If it is still slow, please tell us your iPhone model.

How do I delete everything the game has stored?

Delete the game from your iPhone: that removes your times, settings and recording. If you joined the world board, tap ERASE MY ONLINE TIMES in SETTINGS first: once the game is deleted, the button is gone with it and only an email to us can remove your times there. For anything else held outside your phone, see "How long it is kept, and how to have it deleted" in the privacy policy, or write to us.

Is it safe to ride like this?

No. It is a cartoon. In real life: wear a helmet, stop at red lights, and stay off the sidewalk.
